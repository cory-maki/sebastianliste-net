Sebastian Liste

Documentary Photographer & Founder of PhotoDocumentarians

Sebastian Liste is a documentary photographer whose work has appeared in National Geographic, TIME, The New York Times and The New Yorker. Over twenty years he has built long-term projects across the Americas, the Middle East, Africa, Asia and Europe, and, in parallel, founded PhotoDocumentarians, a mentorship platform that helps documentary photographers bring meaningful projects to publication, exhibition and funding.

The Photographer Behind the Platform

A working practitioner who teaches from experience

Sebastian Liste has spent two decades inside the communities he documents, working at the highest international level while quietly building one of documentary photography’s most substantive teaching practices. His photographs have run in National Geographic, TIME and The New Yorker; his projects have been funded by the Pulitzer Center, the Magnum Foundation and the Getty Images Grant.

That record is the foundation of everything else. A member of NOOR Images and a Leica Ambassador, Sebastian teaches what he has done rather than what he theorizes, and his two decades of documentary work inform every part of the platform he founded.

PhotoDocumentarians is the structured expression of that authority: a mentorship platform and global community built from fifteen years of teaching alongside a full working practice. It is the clearest answer to why he founded PhotoDocumentarians, and it now welcomes photographers through the PhotoDocumentarians community.
